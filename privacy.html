Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Kaldreth mobile game (the "Game"), published by Pseudo Playgrounds ("we", "us"), handles information when you play. By installing or playing the Game you agree to the practices described here.

Kaldreth is an offline-first idle RPG. Your game progress is stored on your device and, if you choose to sign in, in your Google Play cloud save. The Game does not need a server to play and we do not maintain user accounts. The one service we run ourselves is the optional rankings described below.

Information We and Our Partners Process

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game offers optional rewarded ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process device and advertising identifiers (including your Advertising ID), IP address, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland we present a consent request before personalized ads are used; you can change or withdraw your choice at any time via Settings → Privacy → Manage Ad Privacy in the Game. See Google's Privacy & Terms and the AdMob data disclosure.

Google Play Games Services

If you sign in, Google Play Games Services provides authentication and cloud saving of your characters. This involves your Google Play player identifier and the save data you generate. Sign-in is optional; the Game is fully playable without it. See Google's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases (such as the Warden's Mark) are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can grant the item; we never receive or store your payment card or billing details.

Bug Reports

If you use the in-app "Report a Bug" feature, the text you write, the app version, and your operating-system name are sent through Formspree so we can read and act on your report. Please do not include personal information in bug descriptions.

Rankings

The Game has rankings that compare characters. While Settings → Rankings → Appear in the rankings is on, the Game sends the following for each of your characters to our rankings service, where it is shown publicly to other players and may be shown on kaldreth.com:

  • the character's name (or the ranking name you chose for it), title, game mode and appearance;
  • its progress: skill levels and experience, lifetime totals such as gold earned, kills and dungeon clears, and the achievements it holds;
  • which expansions the character can use, so its entries are placed on the right rankings.

Each character is identified by a random code the Game creates. It is not your Google account, your Advertising ID or any device identifier, and it is not linked to them. The rankings service is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to deliver each request. We do not store your IP address; to limit abuse we keep only a scrambled value derived from it that changes every day and cannot be turned back into the address.

Do not use a name that identifies you. We may remove a name or an entry that is offensive or that reports false results. If you report another character's entry, the report records which entry it was and the reason.

Turning the setting off stops the Game from sending anything and deletes the entries of every character on that device. Deleting a character deletes its entry. A hardcore character that is retired after falling keeps a memorial entry (its name and the level it reached) until you turn the setting off. After an entry is deleted we keep a minimal record of its random code with no name and no results, holding only whether its name had been changed, removed or the entry blocked, so that those decisions cannot be undone by deleting and starting again.

Local Game Data

Your characters, progress, settings, and purchase entitlement are stored locally on your device (save files are encrypted). Uninstalling the Game removes local data; deleting a character removes that character's data.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • We do not collect precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking servers.

How Information Is Shared

Information is processed only by the service providers described above (Google AdMob, Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, Formspree for bug reports, and Cloudflare for the rankings), each acting under its own privacy policy. Rankings entries are public, as described above. We do not share data with any other third parties except where required by law.

Children

Kaldreth is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will address it.

Withdrawing Consent (EEA, UK & Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, personalized advertising is served only with your consent, and you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it and does not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before the withdrawal. Non-personalized ads may still be shown after you withdraw.

  • In the Game: open Settings → Privacy → Manage Ad Privacy at any time. The same consent form shown at first launch is presented again, and you can revoke or change your choices there.
  • Google Ads Settings: manage or turn off ad personalization for your Google account at adssettings.google.com.
  • Industry opt-outs: youronlinechoices.com (EU) and optout.aboutads.info.
  • Device controls: reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

If you need help exercising these rights, contact us at the address in the Contact section below.

Data Retention & Your Choices

  • Ad consent: change or withdraw it in Settings → Privacy → Manage Ad Privacy (see Withdrawing Consent).
  • Local data: delete a character in-game, or uninstall the Game to remove all local data.
  • Rankings: turn off Settings → Rankings → Appear in the rankings to stop sending and delete your entries. If you no longer have the Game installed, contact us with the character name and we will remove it.
  • Cloud saves: managed through your Google Play account; you may delete Game data from Google Play Games.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device's Google settings.
  • Requests: to ask about or request deletion of data we hold, contact us below.

Security

Local save files are encrypted on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this Policy or your data can be sent to admin.kaldreth@gmail.com.
