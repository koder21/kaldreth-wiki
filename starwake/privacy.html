Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Starwake mobile game (the "Game"), published by Pseudo Playgrounds ("we", "us"), handles information when you play. By installing or playing the Game you agree to the practices described here.

Starwake is an offline, single-player puzzle game. It has no user accounts and no sign-in of any kind. There are no ads in the Game, not one, not ever. The only Google service the Game uses is Google Play Billing, and only at the moment you choose to buy or restore a purchase.

Information We and Our Partners Process

In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Additional worlds and cosmetic themes are one-time purchases processed by Google Play Billing. A purchase is billed to whichever Google account is already signed into the Play Store app on your device — the same account Play uses to install any app. Starwake does not ask you to sign in separately, and never receives or stores your Google account details, email address, or payment or card information. We receive only a confirmation that a product was bought, which we use to unlock it.

If you reinstall the Game or install it on a new device, Play Billing's restore feature looks up what that same Play Store account already owns and unlocks it again automatically, so you never pay twice. This lookup is handled entirely by Google Play; the Game does not maintain its own record of your purchase history beyond what is stored locally to unlock content on the current device. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Local Game Data

Your level progress, settings, and purchase entitlements are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the Game removes this local data.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We show no advertising of any kind, and use no ad networks.
  • We do not run analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.
  • The Game has no accounts and no sign-in; we do not ask for your name, email address, or contacts.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Outside of processing a purchase through Google Play Billing, the Game makes no network connections at all.

How Information Is Shared

Information is processed only by Google Play Billing, under Google's own privacy policy, and only when you make or restore a purchase. We do not share data with any other third parties except where required by law.

Children

Starwake is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will address it.

Data Retention & Your Choices

  • Local data: uninstall the Game to remove all local progress, settings, and entitlement data from your device.
  • Purchases: your purchase history is held by Google Play under your Google account, not by us; manage it through your Google Play account.
  • Requests: to ask about or request deletion of data we hold, contact us below.

Security

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the limited information described above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
