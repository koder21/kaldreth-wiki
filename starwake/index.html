Starwake

Bring sleeping constellations back to life, one quiet tap at a time.

The sky above you is asleep.

Touch a piece that still holds a light. It slides away into the dark, and everything its path passes wakes up behind it. That is the whole rule.

Every board you finish is a fragment of a picture. Six fragments light a centerpiece. Centerpieces join, and a constellation comes up out of the dark.

In the box

  • One rule, learned in a single tap and never replaced
  • 60 boards in World 1, free, and a complete game on their own
  • No ads. Not one, not ever
  • No timer, no lives, no energy. Undo is free and unlimited
  • A lost board costs the attempt and nothing else
  • Plays entirely offline. No account required
  • One thumb, portrait, on a dark screen

Worlds beyond the first, and Endless, are one-time purchases. Nothing expires, and nothing is taken back.
