Please Advise

Raise the land. Lower your expectations.

In one paragraph

Please Advise is a small god game for Android. You are a finger in the sky, and a tribe below treats every hill you raise as a message, and reads it wrong. You sculpt the land with one drag, send the water where you like, and watch them build on what you leave behind. It is offline, has no ads, no purchases and no timers, and costs one price with updates included.

Facts

  • Platform: Android, played in landscape
  • Price: $4.99, one price, updates included
  • Offline: no internet needed, no account
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no timers
  • Data: collects none (privacy policy)
  • Languages: English, German, Japanese, Spanish (Latin America), Portuguese (Brazil)
