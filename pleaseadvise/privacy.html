Please Advise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy covers the Please Advise Android game, application ID com.pseudoplaygrounds.pleaseadvise (the "Game"), published by Pseudo Playgrounds ("we", "us"). Please Advise is a single-player game that works completely offline. This page is public and needs no account to read.

The short version: the Game collects no personal data, shows no ads, has no in-app purchases, needs no account, and does not have permission to use the internet. Nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Information We Collect

None. We do not collect, receive, or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, device identifiers, or Advertising ID. We operate no game servers and no user accounts.

Data Kept on Your Device

The Game saves your progress and settings as ordinary files in its private app storage on your device. It also writes a short technical log there for troubleshooting. These files stay on the device and we never see them. Uninstalling the Game removes them.

Android backup

Android may back up an app's saved files to your Google account through Android's own backup feature, so your progress can return after a reinstall or on a new phone. That is a feature of your device and your Google account, handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive that backup and cannot read it. The Game asks Android to leave its log files out of the backup. You can switch backup off in your phone's Google settings.

Network Access

The released Game does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data over a network. The only permission it asks for is vibration, used for touch feedback.

Purchases

Please Advise is bought once, up front, in the Google Play Store. The Game contains no in-app purchases, no billing code, and no ads. Your purchase is handled entirely by Google Play, using the account already signed in to the Play Store on your device. We never receive your payment details or email address.

What We Do Not Do

  • We show no advertising and use no ad networks.
  • We run no analytics, crash reporting, or tracking.
  • We do not require an account or ask for personal information.
  • We do not collect your location or your Advertising ID.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, because we have none.

This Website

The Please Advise home page and this policy are static pages on kaldreth.com. They set no cookies, run no analytics, and do not track you.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children, and since the Game collects no information from anyone, there is nothing to collect.

Your Choices and Requests

You can play without signing in or sharing anything. To remove the Game's data from your device, uninstall it. To remove an Android backup, use your Google account's backup controls. For anything else, contact us below and we will help.

Changes to This Policy

If the Game ever changes what it collects or gains network access, we will update this page, including the date above, before the change reaches players.
