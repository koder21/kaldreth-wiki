A terrain-sculpting god game about a tribe that misreads everything you do.

About the game

You are the god. You have one finger. Pull the ground up, push it down, and a small tribe will walk where you let them, build where you level the land, and take every ridge, ditch, and flood as a message. They are usually wrong about what the message was. Their holy text promises a land to the north, and the last line of it is a complaint.

Please Advise is a single-player game for Android, played with one thumb in portrait. It runs entirely offline.

In the box

  • Sculpt terrain with a single pull, and water flows where you shape it
  • Followers with opinions, who read your work wrongly and say so
  • One price, updates included
  • No ads. Not one
  • No in-app purchases
  • No account, no sign-in, no internet needed

This page describes the game as it is being built. Please Advise is a working title and details may change before release.
